How It Works

Up and running in minutes

From sign-up to a managed kitchen in three steps.

1

Set up your household

Create your account, add your people, and share any dietary needs or preferences. Takes about three minutes.

2

Stock your kitchen

Add what's in your pantry, fridge, and freezer — scan barcodes, snap a receipt, speak it, or type. Forage remembers.

3

Plan your week

Forage learns what your household likes and suggests meals from what's already in your kitchen. Your shopping list builds itself from there. Nothing wasted, nothing forgotten.
