Features

Your household. Your community.

Manage your home, connect with neighbours, and build something together.

kitchen

Kitchen Inventory

Track everything in your fridge, freezer, pantry, and spice rack. See what's running low, expiring soon, or ready to grab.

calendar_month

AI Meal Planning

Get personalised weekly meal plans based on what you already have, your family's dietary needs, and seasonal availability.

shopping_cart

Smart Shopping Lists

AI-generated shopping lists that know your meal plan, track what's running low, and learn your buying patterns. Budget estimates included.

groups

Community Network

Connect your household with neighbours and friends. Share surplus produce, coordinate bulk purchases, and support each other locally.

menu_book

Recipe Book

Import recipes from any URL, discover new ones with AI, rate and tag your favourites. Share your best recipes with your community.

search

Ask Anything

A built-in AI assistant that knows your household. Ask what to cook, what's in season, or how to make the most of what you've got.

The Network

Households that grow together

Each home manages its own kitchen. Connect into a community to share surplus, swap recipes, and coordinate purchases.

How It Works

Up and running in minutes

From sign-up to a connected household in three steps.

1

Set up your household

Create your account, add your family members, and tell Forage about dietary needs and preferences. Free to start.

2

Manage your home

Stock your kitchen via barcode, camera, or voice. Plan meals, build shopping lists, and let AI handle the details.

3

Join your community

Connect with neighbours, share what you've got extra, and coordinate together. A stronger household starts with a stronger network.
